## 1. 3D Facial Expression Control Panel

Upon importing and setting up an NTE character, the add-on configures the integrated **NTE Face Rig** (adapted from Jideeh's facial control architecture) with direct on-face controls and expression sliders:

- **Eyebrow & Eyelid Controls**: Direct widgets positioned over the face for precise posing of eyebrows and blinking.
- **Pupil & Eye Tracking**: Dedicated gaze widgets and dynamic drivers.
- **Mouth & Lip Phonemes**: Controllers for mouth opening, smiles, frowns, and speech phonemes.

## 3. Bone Collections & Armature Hierarchy

Neverness to Everness armatures are organized into structured groups within the **Bone Collections** tab (Blender 5.2+) to streamline posing and animation workflows.

<Image src={nteBonecoll} alt="Armature Properties Bone Collections panel for NTE character" loading="lazy" />

### Bone Groups Overview
- **Face**: Eyebrow, eye, eyelid, pupil, and mouth controllers.
- **Torso & Spine**: Spine, chest, neck, and head FK/IK handles.
- **Arms & Hands**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins and finger bone controls.
- **Legs & Feet**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Hair & Clothes**: Secondary dynamic bones for skirts, jackets, ribbons, and hair strands.
- **Lighting**: Vector gizmos and lighting controllers.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the **Bone Collections** tab to solo/isolate a specific bone collection, or click the **eye icons** to toggle visibility while animating.
:::
