## Jideeh's Camera

### How to use

- **Yellow L corners / AIM**: Is scaleable. The camera tracks this widget to aim. If scaled, the FOV will be adjusted.
- **Orange Circle / FOCUS**: Controls the depth of field — where to or how near/far the camera should focus.
- **Purple Circle / PIVOT**: Controls where the camera should orbit.
- **Curved Yellow Line / FREQ**: Controls the frequency of your shake.
- **Vertical Yellow Slider / SHAKE_Y**: Controls the shake intensity of Y axis.
- **Horizontal Yellow Slider / SHAKE_X**: Controls the shake intensity of X axis.
- **The one with the F widget / DOF**: A toggle to depth of field.
- **ROOT**: Master Controller of the camera.
- **CAM (behind the camera)**: Acts like a camera stabilizer. You can move the camera without changing where it's aiming.
