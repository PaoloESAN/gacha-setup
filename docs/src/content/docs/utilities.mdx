## Apply VMD Motion

Available on **Blender 5.0+**; disabled on earlier versions. Select your FBX character's armature, generated Rigify rig, or a mesh bound to it. Choose **Gacha Setup → Utilities → Apply VMD Motion** and select a `.vmd` file. **MMD Tools must be installed**, but may remain disabled. Gacha Setup activates it for the import and restores its previous state afterward.

The importer creates a new action and keeps the previous action. Adjust translation scale (default `0.08`) and the starting frame in the file browser. It sets the scene to 30 FPS and updates the animation range.

Mappings cover humanoid Rigify controls, Genshin `Bip001` bones, common humanoid names, and original MMD names. Unmapped bones are reported. **Matching Facial Morphs** transfers exact shape-key names or an expanded Japanese mapping for mouths, eyes, and brows. Facial-only VMD files are also supported.

Undriven expressions are animated directly. Compatible driven expressions are transferred through their existing controls without removing drivers. Expressions sharing a control channel use the strongest weight per frame; different axes of the same bone remain independent. Facial keys are baked per frame with linear interpolation, and previous actions are preserved. An existing `Mouth_O_manual` is preferred; it is not created automatically, and standard alternatives are tried when absent.

Simple arithmetic drivers use fast calibration verified against Blender, including local-space location limits. Complex drivers retain full calibration. Keyframes are written in batches to avoid updating the scene for each facial key.

To combine separate files, import the body VMD first (disable **Matching Facial Morphs** to use only its motion). Then import the facial VMD with **Facial Only (Preserve Body)** and the same **Start Frame**. This excludes all bone tracks, even from combined files, and preserves body curves, FK/IK switches and FPS. Only matching facial expressions within the imported interval are replaced. The scene range is never shortened; it expands if the face ends later. Bone-based gaze tracks are also excluded in this mode.

Transform controls must use local space; rotation controls require Euler mode. Unresponsive or unsupported drivers are skipped and recorded in the armature properties `setup_wizard_vmd_unmapped_morphs` and `setup_wizard_vmd_face_warnings`. Drivers with multiple controls are calibrated with the others held at their initial pose; complex rigs may need custom mapping. Clothing, hair, and custom skeletons may also need additional mapping. VMD cameras, lights, and physics are not imported.

## Jideeh's Camera

### How to use

- **Yellow L corners / AIM**: Is scaleable. The camera tracks this widget to aim. If scaled, the FOV will be adjusted.
- **Orange Circle / FOCUS**: Controls the depth of field — where to or how near/far the camera should focus.
- **Purple Circle / PIVOT**: Controls where the camera should orbit.
- **Curved Yellow Line / FREQ**: Controls the frequency of your shake.
- **Vertical Yellow Slider / SHAKE_Y**: Controls the shake intensity of Y axis.
- **Horizontal Yellow Slider / SHAKE_X**: Controls the shake intensity of X axis.
- **The one with the F widget / DOF**: A toggle to depth of field.
- **ROOT**: Master Controller of the camera.
- **CAM (behind the camera)**: Acts like a camera stabilizer. You can move the camera without changing where it's aiming.

## Hair Shadows

:::caution[Experimental]
This feature is experimental and may produce unexpected results.

**Hair Shadows cannot be used at the same time as See Eye Through.**
:::

Adds shadows from a character's hair onto their face in Blender 5.2 or newer.

1. In **Object Mode**, select a character mesh or its armature. The character needs materials named Face and Hair or Bangs so the tool can identify the face and hair.
2. In **Gacha Setup → Utilities**, click **Hair Shadows**.
3. Open the **Compositor** and select the character's **Hair Shadows** group. Adjust **Offset X** and **Offset Y** to position the shadow, **Shadow Tint** for its color, and **Strength** for its intensity.

<Image src={hairShadowImg} alt="Hair Shadows compositor group and effect on character face" loading="lazy" />

The tool enables the viewport compositor automatically. Check the result in rendered shading or in a render. Run the tool again on the same character to update its existing effect.
