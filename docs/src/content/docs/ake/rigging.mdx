## 1. 3D Facial Expression Control Panel

Upon importing and rigging an Arknights: Endfield character, the add-on builds the adapted **Isaac Face Rig** with direct on-face manipulation widgets:

- **Eyebrow & Eyelid Controls**: Direct widgets positioned over the face for precise posing of eyebrows and blinking.
- **Pupil & Eye Tracking**: Dedicated gaze widgets and dynamic drivers.
- **Mouth & Lip Phonemes**: Controllers for mouth opening, smiles, frowns, and speech phonemes.

## 2. Hair & Clothes Physics (Damped Track)

The add-on automatically generates a dynamic physics rig using Blender's native **Damped Track** constraints:

1. Enable **Hair & Clothes Physics** in the Setup Settings before running the setup (or apply it afterwards via the Character Settings panel > **Apply Physics**).
2. Adjust the **Hair** and **Clothes** influence sliders in the N-panel to customize the responsiveness and damping strength of the dynamic secondary motion.

---

## 3. Bone Collections & Rig Layers

Character armatures are organized into structured groups within the **Bone Collections / Rig Layers** tab (Blender 5.2+), making it easy to isolate and animate specific body parts.

<Image src={akeBonecoll} alt="Rig Layers panel for Arknights: Endfield character (Face, Torso IK/FK, Fingers, Arm IK/FK, Leg IK/FK, Hair, Clothes)" loading="lazy" />

### Rig Layers Overview

- **Face (Primary / Secondary)**: Eyebrow, eye, eyelid, pupil, nose, and mouth controllers.
- **Torso (IK / FK)**: Spine, chest, neck, and head handles.
- **Arm.L / Arm.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins.
- **Leg.L / Leg.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Fingers (Detail)**: Hand and finger bone controllers.
- **Hair / Clothes / Cage / Other**: Dynamic secondary bones for hair strands, coats, and accessories.
- **Lighting / Tweaks / Pivots & Pins / Offsets / Weapon**: Utility and weapon control layers.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the Rig Layers tab to solo/isolate a specific collection, or click the **eye icons** to toggle visibility while animating.
:::
