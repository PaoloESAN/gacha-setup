## 1. 3D Facial Expression Control Panel

Upon importing and rigging a Wuthering Waves character, the add-on builds a dedicated **Face Rig Panel** equipped with intuitive direct-manipulation widgets and expression preset sliders:

- **Eyes & Brows**: Direct widgets positioned around the face for pupil tracking, squinting, blinking, and eyebrow angling.
- **Mouth Shapes**: Individual lip corner, open mouth, smile, frown, and phoneme controllers.
- **Cutscene Presets**: Organized sliders on the facial board for quickly triggering and blending the character's built-in expression poses.

---

## 2. Hair & Clothes Physics (Damped Track)

The add-on automatically generates a dynamic physics rig using Blender's native **Damped Track** constraints:

1. Enable **Hair & Clothes Physics** in the Setup Settings before running the setup (or apply it afterwards via Advanced Setup > Rig Character).
2. Adjust the **Hair** and **Clothes** influence sliders in the N-panel to customize the responsiveness and damping strength of the dynamic secondary motion.

---

## 3. Bone Collections

Character armatures are organized into structured groups within the **Bone Collections** tab (Blender 5.2+), making it easy to isolate and animate specific body parts.

<Image src={wuwaBonecoll} alt="Armature Properties Bone Collections panel for Wuthering Waves character" loading="lazy" />

### Bone Collections Overview

- **Face**: Eyebrow, eye, eyelid, pupil, nose, and mouth controllers.
- **Torso (IK / FK)**: Spine, chest, neck, and head handles.
- **Arm.L / Arm.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins and pole targets.
- **Leg.L / Leg.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Fingers**: Hand and finger bone controllers.
- **Hair**: Dynamic secondary bones for hair strands and pony tails.
- **Clothes**: Dynamic secondary bones for coats, skirts, capes, and tassels.
- **Lighting**: Vector handles and lighting controllers.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the **Bone Collections** tab to solo/isolate a specific bone collection, or click the **eye icons** to toggle visibility while animating.
:::
