## 1. Controlling Light & Shadow Direction

During character setup, a dedicated `Light Direction_<Character>` object is created in your scene collection.

1. Locate `Light Direction_<Character>` in the **Outliner** panel.
2. Open the **Item Transform** panel in Object Properties.
3. Adjust the **Rotation** values (XYZ Euler) to rotate the light vector:
   - **Rotation Z**: Rotates the sun angle horizontally around the character.
   - **Rotation Y**: Adjusts the horizontal tilt of the light across the character.
   - **Rotation X**: Adjusts the vertical pitch of the shadow line.

## 2. Character Settings (Item Panel)

Select your character mesh or armature and press `N` in the 3D Viewport. Open the **Item** tab to access the **Character Settings** panel, providing real-time controls without having to manually edit shader node trees:

- **Animate Mode**: Temporarily swaps complex toon shaders for lightweight materials to ensure smooth viewport animation playback.
- **Lighting Mode**: Switch between built-in ambient lighting presets or choose Custom to tune individual color channels.
- **Blush**: Adjusts the intensity of the character's facial blush texture.
- **Toggle Resonator Star**: Toggles the visibility/animation driver for the character's Resonator star emblem.
- **Alpha Transparency**: Easily toggle transparency on alpha materials (such as transparent veils, cloth lace, and hair ribbons) between blended transparency and solid rendering.

<Image src={wuwaLight1} alt="Character Settings panel for Wuthering Waves" loading="lazy" />

### Lighting Modes & Presets

You can select from several curated lighting preset modes:

- **Default**: Original game lighting baseline.
- **Sunrise**: Warm, soft morning glow with soft blue-purple shadow tints.
- **Day**: Crisp, balanced daylight illumination.
- **Sunset**: Golden-hour tones with warm amber highlights and rich shadows.
- **Night**: Dim cool moonlight tones.
- **Rainy**: Overcast, diffused atmospheric shading.
- **Custom**: Unlocks granular control over Ambient, Light, Shadow, and Rim color swatches.

### Custom Colors (Custom Mode)

When **Lighting Mode** is set to **Custom**, the **Custom Colors** subpanel appears to let you fine-tune each channel of the Gustling Waters shader:

- **Ambient**: Controls the unshaded base ambient illumination color.
- **Light**: Color tint applied to fully lit regions.
- **Shadow**: Tone applied to shaded/occluded regions.
- **Rim**: Color of the specular rim light highlight.

<Image src={wuwaLight4} alt="Custom mode panel showing Custom Colors palette, Toggle Resonator Star, and Alpha Transparency" loading="lazy" />

### Resonator Star & Alpha Transparency

- **Toggle Resonator Star**: One-click button to toggle the visibility and animation driver for the character's Resonator star emblem.
- **Alpha Transparency**: Checkbox to toggle transparency on alpha-based materials (such as transparent veils, cloth lace, and hair ribbons) between blended transparency and solid rendering.

### Animate Mode

For smooth and lag-free viewport animation playback, click **Enable Animate Mode** in the Character Settings panel. This temporarily swaps complex toon shader trees with lightweight low-overhead materials while you animate, and you can switch back with one click when preparing your final render.
