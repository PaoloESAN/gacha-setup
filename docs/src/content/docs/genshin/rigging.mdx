## 1. 3D Facial Expression Control Panel

A custom 3D driver board is placed behind the character head to manipulate facial shape keys effortlessly during posing or animation.

<Image src={genshinRigpanel} alt="3D Facial Rig control panel for brows, eyes, and mouth shapes" loading="eager" />

### Control Sections
- **Brows**: Dedicated sliders and bone widgets to trigger anger, sadness, surprise, and brow tilt shape keys.
- **Eyes**: Eyeball tracking gizmos, eye squinting, blink triggers, and shape key blend sliders. Eye drivers feature dynamic Y-location bounds to prevent eye mesh clipping or popping.
- **Mouth**: Expression bone sliders for open mouth, smile, frown, O-shape, and custom mouth phonemes.

## 2. Bone Collections

The add-on automatically categorizes character bones into organized groups.

<Image src={genshinBonecoll} alt="Armature Properties Bone Collections panel" loading="lazy" />

### Bone Groups Overview
- **Face**: Eyebrow, eye, nose, mouth, and cheek controllers.
- **Torso (IK / FK)**: Spine, chest, neck, and head FK/IK handles.
- **Arm.L / Arm.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins and pole targets.
- **Leg.L / Leg.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Fingers & Fingers (Detail)**: Individual digit bone controls for hand posing.
- **Hair**: Secondary dynamic bones for hair strand animation.
- **Clothes**: Secondary dynamic bones for skirt, cape, and ribbon animation.
- **Lighting**: Custom gizmos for adjusting light vectors and Fresnel panels.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the **Bone Collections** tab to isolate a bone collection, making only that collection visible, or the **eye icons** to show or hide bone groups while animating.
:::
