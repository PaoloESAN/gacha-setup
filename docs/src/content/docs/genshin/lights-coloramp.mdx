## 1. Controlling Light & Shadow Direction

During character setup, a dedicated `Light Direction_<Character>` object is created in your scene collection.

1. Locate `Light Direction_<Character>` in the **Outliner** panel (e.g. `Light Direction_Charlotte`).
2. Open the **Item Transform** panel in Object Properties.
3. Adjust the **Rotation** values (XYZ Euler) to rotate the light vector:
   - **Rotation Z**: Rotates the sun angle horizontally around the character.
   - **Rotation Y**: Adjusts the horizontal tilt of the light across the character.
   - **Rotation X**: Adjusts the vertical pitch of the shadow line.

## 2. Character Settings (Item Panel)

Select your character mesh or armature and press `N` in the 3D Viewport. Open the **Item** tab to access the **Character Settings** panel, allowing real-time adjustments without digging into the Shader Editor:

<Image src={genshinLight} alt="Character Settings panel for Genshin Impact in the Item tab" loading="lazy" />

### Lighting Mode
- **Lighting Presets**: Quickly toggle between environmental lighting presets (*Default*, *Sunrise*, *Day*, *Sunset*, *Night*, *Rainy*).
- **Custom Mode**: Selecting the **Custom** mode reveals a custom color palette subpanel to manually fine-tune individual channels: **Ambient**, **Sharp Lit**, **Soft Lit**, **Sharp Shadow**, **Soft Shadow**, **Rim Lit**, and **Rim Shadow**.

### Fresnel Options
- **Use Fresnel**: Checkbox to toggle the outer rim glow effect on or off.
- **Fresnel Color**: Color selector to customize the rim glow tint.
- **Fresnel Power**: Adjusts the strength and intensity of the Fresnel effect.
- **Fresnel Scaler**: Controls the edge falloff scale and thickness.

### Shadow & Scene Settings
- **Shadow Position**: Slider to calibrate the shadow threshold and placement across the character.
- **Catch Shadows**: Enable this option to cast more self-shadows across the character's own mesh.
- **Day / Night**: Slider controlling the balance and transition between daytime and nighttime lighting.
