## 1. Controlling Light & Shadow Direction

During character setup, a dedicated `Light Direction_<Character>` object is created in your scene collection.

1. Locate `Light Direction_<Character>` in the **Outliner** panel (e.g. `Light Direction_Charlotte`).
2. Open the **Item Transform** panel in Object Properties.
3. Adjust the **Rotation** values (XYZ Euler) to rotate the light vector:
   - **Rotation Z**: Rotates the sun angle horizontally around the character.
   - **Rotation Y**: Adjusts the horizontal tilt of the light across the character.
   - **Rotation X**: Adjusts the vertical pitch of the shadow line.

## 2. Interactive 3D Lighting & Coloramp Panel

Every setup includes a 3D control board generated behind or beside your character in the viewport, allowing real-time adjustment of Festivity shader parameters without digging into node trees.

<Image src={genshinLightpanel} alt="3D Lighting and Fresnel control board in viewport" loading="lazy" />

:::note
Some of the light controls on the panel may not work as expected. Sorry about that — this is a known limitation that will be improved in future releases.
:::

### Lighting Controls
- **Soft Lit & Soft Shadow**: Color wheels controlling soft gradient transition tones.
- **Sharp Lit & Sharp Shadow**: Color wheels controlling sharp anime cel-shadow boundaries.
- **Ambient**: Adjusts unshaded base ambient brightness and tint.
- **Rim Lit & Rim Shadow**: Controls rim light highlights and edge shadow intensity.
- **Shadow Offset**: Slider controlling global shadow threshold placement across the character body.

### Fresnel Controls
- **Fresnel Toggle**: Switch on/off the outer rim glow effect.
- **Fresnel Color**: Color wheel for custom rim glow tinting.
- **Fresnel Size**: Slider controlling edge falloff thickness.

To enable fresnel, select the object, go to the **Shader Editor**, then locate the **Global Properties** node and double-click to enter it:

<Image src={genshinFresnel1} alt="Fresnel settings in Global Properties" loading="lazy" />

Then enable it by setting **Use Fresnel** to `1`:

<Image src={genshinFresnel2} alt="Use Fresnel enabled" loading="lazy" />

You can change the color and intensity from the **Fresnel Color** in the light panel, adjusting its intensity with **Fresnel Power**.
