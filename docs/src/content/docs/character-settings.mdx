# Character Settings

The **Character Settings** panel is located in the **Item** tab of the 3D Viewport sidebar (`N` panel) when a character armature or mesh is selected. It provides real-time control over shader lighting, anime outlines, Fresnel rim effects, shadow behavior, and physics directly without opening shader node graphs.

<Image src={characterSettingsImg} alt="Character Settings Panel in Item Tab" loading="eager" />

---

## Animate Mode

**Animate Mode** enables smooth, lightweight viewport playback by temporarily swapping heavy multi-layer toon materials with lightweight alternatives and hiding outline modifiers.

- Click **Enable Animate Mode** when working on animation keyframes and pacing.
- Click **Disable Animate Mode** prior to rendering or staging final lighting.

## Lighting Type & Lighting Mode

You can control character lighting either via the **3D Lighting Panel** gizmo in the scene or through the **Character Settings** UI presets.

### 3D Lighting Panel
When **Lighting Type** is set to **Lighting Panel**, three-dimensional color wheels and directional controllers in the 3D Viewport drive the character's key light, ambient light, and shadow colors interactively.

<Image src={lightPanelImg} alt="3D Lighting Panel in the viewport" loading="lazy" />

To adjust the values, enter **Pose Mode**, select a panel control, and press `G` to move it.

### Lighting Presets (This Panel)

## Light & Shadow Direction (Light Direction Object)

During setup, a dedicated empty object named `Light Direction_<Character>` (e.g., `Light Direction_Charlotte`) is generated in the scene collection to control where the sun and shadows strike the model.

<Image src={genshinLightdir} alt="Genshin Impact Light Direction object in Outliner and Item Transform" loading="lazy" />

1. Select `Light Direction_<Character>` from the 3D Viewport or **Outliner**.
2. Open the **Item Transform** panel in the sidebar (`N` key).
3. Adjust the **Rotation** values (XYZ Euler):
   - **Rotation Z**: Rotates the sun angle azimuth horizontally around the character.
   - **Rotation Y**: Adjusts the lateral tilt of the light vector across the character.
   - **Rotation X**: Adjusts the vertical pitch and elevation angle of the shadow terminator.

## Fresnel & Outlines

### Fresnel
Fresnel adds an edge rim/specular glow characteristic of high-end cel-shading:
- **Toggle Fresnel**: Enable or disable the effect.
- **Fresnel Color**: Tint of the outer rim.
- **Fresnel Power**: Falloff sharpness across curved surfaces.
- **Fresnel Scaler**: Overall intensity multiplier.

<Image src={fresnelImg} alt="Fresnel rim effect controls and preview" loading="lazy" />

### Outlines
- **Enable Outlines**: Quickly toggle inverted-hull outline modifiers across all character meshes.
- **Enable Night Soul**: (Natlan Characters in Genshin Impact) Toggles special glowing energy outlines.

---

## Shadow & Scene Settings

- **Shadow Position**: Slides the light termination threshold on curved surfaces.
- **Catch Shadows**: Allows or ignores external scene shadows cast onto the character.
- **Day / Night**: Blends between daytime and nighttime texture ramp calibrations.
- **Blush Strength**: Modulates facial blush intensity where supported.

---

## Hair & Clothes Physics

You can interactively apply or fine-tune dynamic physics without re-running the setup wizard:

- **Hair Physics Slider**: Adjusts Damped Track springiness and physics influence on hair strands.
- **Clothes Physics Slider**: Adjusts dynamic movement on clothing and skirt accessories.
- **Apply Physics / Remove Physics**: Add or remove the constraint system at any time.
