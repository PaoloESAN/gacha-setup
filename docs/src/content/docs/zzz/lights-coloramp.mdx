## 1. Controlling Light & Shadow Direction

During character setup, a dedicated `Light Direction_<Character>` object is created in your scene collection.

1. Locate `Light Direction_<Character>` in the **Outliner** panel.
2. Open the **Item Transform** panel in Object Properties.
3. Adjust the **Rotation** values (XYZ Euler) to rotate the light vector:
   - **Rotation Z**: Rotates the sun angle horizontally around the character.
   - **Rotation Y**: Adjusts the horizontal tilt of the light across the character.
   - **Rotation X**: Adjusts the vertical pitch of the shadow line.

## 2. Kythera Shader: Character Settings (Item Panel)

When using **Kythera's Shader**, select your character and open the **Item** tab in the 3D Viewport sidebar (`N` key) to access real-time shader controls.

### Lighting Modes & Presets

Choose from preset lighting environments or switch to Custom mode:

- **Default**: Standard game look.
- **Sunrise**: Soft morning light with pastel shadows.
- **Day**: Crisp daytime illumination.
- **Sunset**: Warm golden hour tones.
- **Night**: Dim moonlight palette.
- **Rainy**: Diffuse overcast lighting.
- **Custom**: Unlocks granular control over tints, shadows, and rim light.

<Image src={zzzLight1} alt="Kythera Shader Lighting Mode presets in Zenless Zone Zero" loading="lazy" />

### Shading & Tints (Custom Mode)

When set to **Custom**, you can fine-tune:

- **Ambient**: Base ambient color tone.
- **Lit Tint / Lit Brightness**: Direct illumination color and brightness intensity.
- **Shadow Tint / Shadow Intensity**: Cel-shadow color tint and darkness depth.
- **Fake SSS Intensity**: Adjusts the soft Subsurface Scattering (SSS) falloff simulation across skin and fabric.

<Image src={zzzLight2} alt="Shading and Tints settings in Character Settings for ZZZ" loading="lazy" />

### Rim Light Settings

Customize the edge highlight effect around your character:

- **Enable Rim Light**: Toggles the rim lighting effect.
- **Color**: Tint of the rim light glow.
- **Coverage**: How far the rim light extends across the mesh silhouettes.
- **Brightness**: Overall intensity of the rim light.
- **Left / Right & Up / Down**: Fine positional vectors to shift the highlight direction.
