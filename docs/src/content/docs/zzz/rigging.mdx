## 1. Jideeh Face Rig

Upon importing and setting up a Zenless Zone Zero character, the add-on configures the **jideeh ZZZ Face Rig** integrated directly inside the character's main armature for advanced facial expression control.

<Image src={zzzFacerig} alt="jideeh ZZZ Face Rig 3D control board and character rig" loading="eager" />

### Key Facial Controls & Features

- **jideeh Facial Rig Integration**: Advanced drivers for eyebrows, eyelids, eye tracking, and mouth shape keys.
- **On-Face Control Widgets**: Allows intuitive posing of individual facial features directly in the 3D viewport.

## 2. Bone Collections

Zenless Zone Zero armatures are organized into structured groups within the **Bone Collections** tab (Blender 5.2+).

<Image src={zzzBonecoll} alt="Armature Properties Bone Collections panel for Zenless Zone Zero character" loading="lazy" />

### Bone Collections Overview

- **Face**: Eyebrow, eye, eyelid, pupil, nose, and mouth controllers.
- **Torso (IK / FK)**: Spine, chest, neck, head, and breast/torso dynamics.
- **Arm.L / Arm.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins and pole targets.
- **Leg.L / Leg.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Fingers**: Hand and finger bone controllers.
- **Hair**: Dynamic secondary bones for hair strands and tails.
- **Clothes**: Dynamic secondary bones for jackets, skirts, and accessories.
- **Lighting**: Vector handles and lighting controllers.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the **Bone Collections** tab to solo/isolate a specific bone collection, or click the **eye icons** to toggle visibility while animating.
:::
