## 1. 3D Facial Expression Control Board & Isaac Face Rig

Upon importing and setting up the character, the add-on configures two main rigs: the normal body rig and the secondary **Isaac Face Rig** placed near the head for controlling facial expressions.

<Image src={hsrFacerig} alt="Isaac Face Rig 3D control board and character rig" loading="eager" />

<Image src={hsrTworigs} alt="Normal body rig and Isaac face rig structure in Blender" loading="lazy" />

### Facial Control Modes

- **On-Face Manual Controls**: The control widgets placed directly over the eyes, brows, and mouth allow you to manipulate individual facial expressions manually.

<Image src={hsrManualrig} alt="Manual control widgets positioned directly on the character face" loading="lazy" />

- **Side Preset Shapekey Sliders**: The sliders located on the sides of the head are used to easily trigger and blend the character's built-in preset facial poses.

<Image src={hsrShapekeys} alt="Side sliders for triggering and blending built-in preset shape keys" loading="lazy" />

## 2. Bone Collections

Honkai: Star Rail character armatures are organized into structured groups within the **Bone Collections** tab (Blender 5.2+).

<Image src={hsrBonecoll} alt="Armature Properties Bone Collections panel for Honkai Star Rail character" loading="lazy" />

### Bone Collections Overview

- **Face**: Eyebrow, eye, eyelid, pupil, nose, and mouth controllers.
- **Torso (IK / FK)**: Spine, chest, neck, and head handles.
- **Arm.L / Arm.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right arm chains with individual IK target pins and pole targets.
- **Leg.L / Leg.R (IK & FK)**: Left and right leg chains with foot roll and knee target controls.
- **Fingers**: Hand and finger bone controllers.
- **Hair**: Dynamic secondary bones for hair strands and pony tails.
- **Clothes**: Dynamic secondary bones for coats, skirts, capes, and tassels.
- **Lighting**: Vector handles and lighting controllers.

:::tip
Use the **star icon** in the **Bone Collections** tab to solo/isolate a specific bone collection, or click the **eye icons** to toggle visibility while animating.
:::
